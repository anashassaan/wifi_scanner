Privacy Policy for WiFi QR Password Scanner

Effective Date: May 30, 2026

WiFi QR Password Scanner ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data (Crucial for WiFi Scanning)

To discover, measure, and connect to nearby WiFi networks, Android requires apps to access location services. WiFi Scanner requires precise and approximate location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) uniquely to interface with your device's wireless hardware. This data is processed locally on your device in real-time, is strictly used for core app functionality, and is never stored, tracked, or shared with third parties or external servers.

Camera Access

The App requires access to your device's camera to scan QR codes. This access is only active when you are using the scanning feature. Images from your camera are processed in real-time on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

No Personal Identification

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identity data.

2. Data Storage

All scanned WiFi credentials and history are stored locally on your device using an encrypted or secure local database. We do not have access to this data.

3. Offline Functionality

WiFi QR Password Scanner works completely offline. No internet connection is required to scan or view your history, and no data is shared with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Security

We prioritize the security of your data stored on your device. However, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device itself.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
